## The finding I did not expect

**Closing four coverage gaps immediately surfaced five more.** c27-O2 identified aloud/hunk/browse/
notebook as "the" undocumented channels and I closed them in the Phase A pass. This batch drew
image, store, zettel, github, skill — all with no entry. My c27 framing treated the gap as a fixed
list; it is better modelled as a *class-wide* gap: format channels are well covered, while
**constructor** (image, skill, agent) and **delivery-mechanism** (store, zettel, github) channels
are systematically uncovered.

That reframing matters for what to do next: organising by channel class would close the gap in two
passes, whereas one-channel-at-a-time will keep rediscovering it indefinitely. I should have
inferred this in c27 — the three-way channel taxonomy (nn 20260717011133-2169) was already
recorded, and format channels being the covered ones follows directly from them being the ones
shuffle draws most often.
